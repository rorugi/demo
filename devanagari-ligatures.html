    How this comparison was made

    The board labels each font's rendering with one of three words: half form (the first consonant sits beside the second without its stem), ligature (one combined glyph, joined or stacked) or visible virama (no combined form, so the ् stays).

    “Different” is not a judgement by eye. Every example was shaped in both fonts with HarfBuzz beforehand and the resulting glyph outlines compared. The page itself renders everything live in your browser with the same fonts from Google Fonts.
