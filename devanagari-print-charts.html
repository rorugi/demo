Printing

  • Use the print button on the chart or your browser's print command. Keep the scale at 100 % so the sheet fills the page.
  • Your choices (fonts, traditions, orientation) are kept in the link, so you can bookmark or share a chart set up the way you like it.
  • To learn the letters and conjuncts behind the charts, see Devanagari writing and Devanagari ligatures.
