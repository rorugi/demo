This week

How this site makes it stick

  1. Retrieval, not rereading. Every card makes you produce the answer first: type the transliteration, write the letter, recall the ending.
  2. Spacing. Cards you know drift out to days and weeks; cards you miss return in a minute. Ten to twenty minutes a day beats a weekend cram.
  3. Small batches. The book suggests about ten new characters a session. New cards are capped per day (change it in Settings).
  4. Interleaving. Drills mix topics so you learn to recognise which rule applies, as in an exam.
  5. Understanding first. The sound system and the verb/noun road maps explain why forms look as they do, so there is less to memorise.

About this tool

Version

    A study companion for A. M. Ruppel, The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit (Cambridge University Press, 2017), Chapters 1–40. It is meant to get the material into long-term memory and to prepare you for exams on it.

    What it does

    • Learn — the chapters condensed on one page, with exam traps and self-test questions.
    • Review — flashcards scheduled with FSRS-5 (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler by the open-spaced-repetition project, default parameters). Each card comes back when your predicted recall drops to your target (90% by default; change it in Settings).
    • Drill — unlimited generated questions, mixed across topics.
    • Mock exam — timed, auto-marked papers; each one is stored with your progress.
    • Overview — every knowledge nugget as a circle, so you see where you stand.
    • Marks and comments — colour any section of the notes by how well you know it, and select any text to comment on it; see them all under ☰ → Comments and marks.

    Install as an app

    On Android and desktop Chrome/Edge: ☰ menu → Install app (or the install icon in the address bar). On iPhone/iPad (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. The app then starts like any other, also offline; your progress stays the same.

    Your data

    Progress stays in your browser, syncs to your Claude account on the claude.ai page, can be saved to or loaded from a file in the ☰ menu, and — if you connect one — syncs to your own PHP server. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
