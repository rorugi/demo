Learn to write Devanagari अ आ क

Tap a letter and watch it being written, stroke by stroke. The numbers show where each stroke starts.

Strokes

    The grid has four zones: vowel signs such as i and e above, two rows for the letter, u, ū and ṛ below; the head line runs along the top of the letter and is written last. Vowel signs are shown on a dotted circle that stands for the letter they go with; the circle is not written. The stroke order follows the common school variant.
