अक्षरलोकः
Akṣaraloka
World of letters
A small game for learning to read Sanskrit. Everything in this world is written in Devanagari: walls, water, animals and doors. Walk through its rooms, read what you find, collect letters and name the creatures that stand in your way. What you learn fades over time, so the world brings it back to you.
